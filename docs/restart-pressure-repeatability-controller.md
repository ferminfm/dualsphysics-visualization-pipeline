# Frozen repeatability source and successor controller

The first successor experiment must preserve the exact current CFD source,
binary, material configuration and original comparator. The predecessor gate
hardcodes its closed batch ID; its source-HEAD check otherwise couples a batch
name-only controller migration to a new science build identity.

A narrowly bound diagnostic route separates `source_commit` (the unchanged
6fd43a5960954ddb2ea606d80c8405c224e190ba science/build) from an explicit committed
`control_source_commit`. It permits only a single fresh diagnostic through
t-star 1.1, the exact frozen binary and source-bundle hashes, a descendant
whose only changed paths are this gate, its two tests and this note. It cannot
authorize a restore, production, another CFD source, an altered build, or a
synthetic claim. All material hashes, committed regular files, original
criteria, observed gate tests, source-bound permits and immediate prelaunch
checks remain mandatory. Original source and build manifests are not rewritten.

The successor global ceilings are tightened to 16 total scientific starts and
10 full-resolution starts. The outer serial supervisor additionally protects
six complete final-set roles. No compiler or solver is invoked by this change.
