## Profile 2: CFD / Domain Evaluation

### Short Positioning

CFD and fluid-mechanics evaluator who reviews AI-generated technical answers for physics consistency, boundary-condition logic, nondimensional reasoning, numerical-method limits, visualization support, and overclaim risk.

### Longer Positioning

I evaluate scientific-computing outputs by checking whether the answer respects the governing physics and the evidence actually available. I look for boundary-condition mismatches, missing fields, resolution-sensitive conclusions, unsupported morphology claims, and visualization artifacts. The goal is practical: identify what is supported, what is only exploratory, and what exact evidence would be needed for a stronger statement.

### Best Supporting Evidence

- CFD evaluator sample packet with self-authored examples and rubric scoring.
- Basilisk internal-nozzle and jet benchmark evidence packets showing pressure/profile, morphology, convergence, and public-claim boundaries.
- OpenFOAM and DualSPHysics public pages that distinguish visualization demos from stronger physical claims.
- Review-packet discipline: public/private artifact separation, no unsupported pressure panels, no fit-readiness language without convergence evidence.

### Use Cases

- CFD/domain-expert AI response evaluation.
- Rubric design for scientific-computing answer quality.
- Claim-safety review for simulation and visualization outputs.
- Technical feedback that is specific enough to guide the next experiment or extraction.

## Claim-Risk Checklist

Before public use, check that the copy:

- does not imply employment or affiliation with a named company unless independently true and approved;
- does not imply platform acceptance or assessment success;
- does not present exploratory simulations as solver validation;
- does not describe visualization demos as production CFD;
- does not imply unsupported atomisation, pressure-nozzle, or final model-fit conclusions;
- does not expose private local paths, raw solver outputs, or unpublished data;
- separates public evidence from internal review artifacts;
- keeps the user positioned as a computational scientist and evaluator, not as an official representative of any platform.
