# Public Use Boundary

## What This Is

This is a self-authored public-safe sample packet for CFD/domain-expert AI evaluation. The examples are generic and are derived from common scientific-computing review patterns: boundary-condition mismatch, missing field evidence, convergence caveats, visualization overclaiming, and nondimensional comparison errors.

## What This Is Not

- Not proprietary assessment content.
- Not live-platform material.
- Not an application submission.
- Not proof of employment, acceptance, or contracting status with any organization.
- Not validation of any solver, CFD workflow, or visualization.
- Not evidence of production CFD readiness.
- Not a final atomisation, breakup, pressure-nozzle, or reduced-model fit claim.

## Public-Safe Framing

Safe wording:

> Self-authored CFD evaluator samples showing how I review AI-generated scientific-computing answers for physics consistency, numerical evidence, visualization support, and claim boundaries.

Unsafe wording:

> Official platform answers, assessment keys, validated atomisation results, or proof of acceptance by a specific evaluation platform.

## Human Review Gate

Before linking this packet publicly, a human reviewer should confirm:

- examples do not resemble proprietary tests;
- no private local paths or unpublished data are exposed;
- scoring is presented as evaluator style, not a certification;
- claim boundaries remain visible.
