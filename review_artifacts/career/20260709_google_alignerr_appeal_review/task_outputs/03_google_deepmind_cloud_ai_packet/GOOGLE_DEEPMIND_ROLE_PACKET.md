# Google Cloud AI / DeepMind-Style Role Packet

## Positioning Thesis

I should be presented as a PhD computational scientist who builds source-first scientific-computing workflows, evaluates AI/scientific outputs with domain rigor, and communicates complex numerical evidence clearly. The strongest fit is research-engineering-adjacent AI-for-science and scientific AI evaluation, not a claim of prior Google/DeepMind employment or frontier-model training.

## Evidence Narrative

- **Research engineering:** VisualBasilisk demonstrates a small, tested bridge layer with manifests, schema discipline, CLI checks, dry-run render plans, and explicit fixture policy.
- **AI-for-science adjacency:** Basilisk, OpenFOAM, DualSPHysics, and reduced jet-model artifacts show the ability to reason about simulations, physical assumptions, and evidence quality.
- **Responsible AI/evaluation:** The work repeatedly separates public claims from internal evidence, blocks unsupported pressure panels, and labels non-validation boundaries.
- **Technical education:** Public site pages and documentation translate scientific workflows for readers without asking them to inspect raw solver outputs.

## Role-Fit Summary

For Google DeepMind-style research engineering, emphasize implementation discipline, mathematical/numerical reasoning, and the ability to bridge theory, code, and evaluation.

For Google Cloud AI/applied AI education, emphasize source-first tooling, reproducible demos, public-safe documentation, and developer-facing explanation.

For AI-for-science teams, emphasize computational modeling background, scientific visualization, hypothesis/evidence gates, and refusal to overclaim.
