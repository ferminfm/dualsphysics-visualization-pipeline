1. **Research engineering:** I can turn a scientific workflow into source, schemas, tests, and reviewable artifacts rather than one-off demos.
2. **AI-for-science:** My CFD/VOF/SPH work is about evidence quality: boundary conditions, numerical assumptions, diagnostics, and visualization limits.
3. **Responsible AI:** I build processes that prevent overclaiming, label private vs public artifacts, and block unsupported outputs such as pressure panels with zero-range evidence.
4. **Cloud/applied AI education:** I can explain technical systems to developers and scientific users through pages, runbooks, and reproducible smoke tests.
5. **Evaluation:** I can evaluate model outputs in fluid mechanics by checking physics, numerics, assumptions, uncertainty, and whether visual evidence supports the text.
6. **Cross-functional communication:** I produce Markdown/JSON handoffs, decision checklists, and conservative public copy that Layer 1/human reviewers can inspect.
