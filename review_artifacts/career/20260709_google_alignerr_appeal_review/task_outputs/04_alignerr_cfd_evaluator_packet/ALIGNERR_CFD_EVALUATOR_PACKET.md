# Alignerr-Style CFD Domain-Expert Evaluator Packet

## Ethical Scope

This packet is a self-authored preparation artifact for CFD/domain-expert AI evaluation work. It is not based on proprietary Alignerr tests, confidential prompts, or live assessment materials. It should be used to show evaluation style, not to bypass platform rules.

## Evaluator Profile

The user's strongest evaluator profile is: PhD computational scientist with hands-on CFD/SPH/VOF/OpenFOAM/Basilisk/DualSPHysics evidence workflows, able to review AI-generated technical answers for physical correctness, numerical-method plausibility, boundary-condition consistency, visualization support, and overclaim risk.

## Evaluation Strengths

- Fluid mechanics: jets, free surfaces, VOF/SPH interpretation, nondimensional reasoning, and boundary-condition sanity.
- Numerical methods: mesh/resolution sensitivity, timestep/stability, convergence caveats, solver-output limitations.
- Visualization evidence: detecting unsupported claims from images, one-cell debris, projection artifacts, missing fields, or misleading overlays.
- Scientific communication: translating a review into clear pass/warn/fail criteria with evidence.
- Claim safety: refusing validation, production-CFD, experimental-agreement, and fit-ready language when evidence is insufficient.

## Evaluator Positioning Statement

I evaluate CFD and scientific-computing AI outputs by asking whether the answer respects the governing physics, boundary conditions, nondimensional scales, numerical limitations, and evidence actually present in the data or visualization. I separate plausible exploratory reasoning from validated claims and give actionable corrections without inventing unsupported results.
