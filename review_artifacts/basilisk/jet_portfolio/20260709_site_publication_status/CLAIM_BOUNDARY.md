- Basilisk two-phase VOF benchmark visualization workflow.
- Official circular route as the lead visual sample.
- `C1_rect_area_top_hat` only as a secondary 2:1 rectangular top-hat imposed-inlet comparison.

- The rectangular route is not Poiseuille.

- Pressure visualization remains blocked until valid nonzero runtime pressure export exists.
- Q/lambda2 remain blocked pending validated gradient-tensor export.
- Fit-ready reduced-model data are not claimed.

- validation
- production CFD
- experimental agreement
- atomisation prediction
- pressure-nozzle modeling

- public proof of breakup physics beyond the bounded benchmark evidence
