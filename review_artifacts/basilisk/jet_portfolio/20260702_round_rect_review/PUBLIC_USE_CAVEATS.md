# Public Use Caveats

Allowed only after human publication review:

- two-phase VOF benchmark visualization;
- solver-derived interface/surface postprocessing;
- conservative round-vs-rectangular imposed-inlet comparison;
- scalar diagnostics for phase, speed, and vorticity magnitude.

Prohibited:

- validation;
- production CFD;
- experimental agreement;
- atomisation prediction;
- pressure-nozzle modeling;
- fit readiness;
- pressure visualization from zero-range restored pressure;
- describing the selected rectangular route as Poiseuille or internal-nozzle flow.
