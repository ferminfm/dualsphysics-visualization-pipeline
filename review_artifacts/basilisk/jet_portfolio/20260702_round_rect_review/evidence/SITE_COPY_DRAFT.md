# Site Copy Draft

## Title

Basilisk two-phase jet benchmark visualization

## Short summary

A conservative research-workflow sample that turns existing Basilisk two-phase VOF benchmark outputs into compact scalar diagnostics and Blender-rendered interface-surface review assets. The official circular route is the lead example; a 2:1 rectangular top-hat imposed-inlet route is shown only as a secondary caveated comparison.

## What is not claimed

This page does not claim validation, production CFD, atomisation prediction, pressure-nozzle modeling, fit readiness, or experimental agreement.

## Captions

- Official circular route: solver-derived interface surface rendered for internal review.
- Round vs rectangular: secondary imposed-inlet comparison with matched caveats.
- Scalar diagnostics: phase, speed, and diagnostic vorticity views; pressure omitted because valid nonzero pressure export is not available.
