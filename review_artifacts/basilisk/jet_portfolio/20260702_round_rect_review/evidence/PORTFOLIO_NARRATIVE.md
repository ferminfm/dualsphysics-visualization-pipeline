# Portfolio Narrative

This project packages a Basilisk two-phase VOF benchmark as a conservative scientific-visualization sample. The lead route is the official circular outlet/control case. A secondary route compares a 2:1 rectangular top-hat imposed inlet (`C1_rect_area_top_hat`) to show how the postprocessing and review workflow handles a non-circular imposed-inlet comparison.

The work emphasizes solver-derived interface surfaces, scalar diagnostics from real available fields, compact review media, and Blender/VisualBasilisk-style postprocessing. It does not claim validation, production CFD, experimental agreement, atomisation prediction, pressure-nozzle modeling, or fit readiness.
