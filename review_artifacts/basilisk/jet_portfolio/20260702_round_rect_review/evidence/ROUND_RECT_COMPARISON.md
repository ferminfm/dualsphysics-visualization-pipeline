# Round-vs-Rectangular Comparison

## Conservative conclusion

The official circular route remains the lead visual/scientific sample for the portfolio package. The rectangular route is useful as a secondary caveated comparison only.

## Labels

- Official circular control: lead route.
- `C1_rect_area_top_hat`: secondary `2:1 rectangular top-hat imposed-inlet comparison`.

## Caveats

- The selected rectangular route is not Poiseuille.
- The selected rectangular route does not resolve internal-nozzle flow.
- The comparison is not validation, production CFD, atomisation prediction, fit-ready evidence, or pressure-nozzle modeling.
- Pressure panels are blocked; scalar media use phase, speed, vorticity magnitude, and ambient context only.
