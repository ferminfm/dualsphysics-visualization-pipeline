# Public narrative draft — not approved for publication

This internal portfolio study compares several Basilisk two-phase VOF routes:
an official circular benchmark, a caveated rectangular top-hat comparison, a
tested quarter-domain acceleration, and a pressure-driven full-domain internal
nozzle. The quarter route was rejected for quantitative continuation after a
matched baseline missed the exit-flow and near-exit geometry gates. The bounded
internal-nozzle L7 reference produced usable uninterrupted scalar, geometry,
native-interface, and runtime pressure evidence, while its restored-field
cadence remained unsuitable for exact-time comparison. In the tested window the
liquid structure remained connected; the evidence does not support atomisation
or breakup claims.

Publication remains human-gated. This draft does not claim validation,
experimental agreement, convergence, model fit, predictive readiness, or
production readiness.
