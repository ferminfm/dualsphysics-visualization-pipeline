# Portfolio asset selection

The selection contains eight compact, non-repetitive artifacts. Existing
tracked assets are referenced rather than duplicated. New packet proxies are
small copies of task-local evidence; vector summaries are deterministic
derivatives of the cited source tables.

| ID | Role | Artifact | Why retained | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `official-circular-lead-v31` | Lead visual | Existing V3.1 circular sequence | Clearest lead surface narrative | Two-phase VOF benchmark visualization; not validation or production CFD |
| `round-rect-split-v31` | Geometry comparison | Existing synchronized round/rectangular split screen | Shows geometry contrast without duplicating the lead | Rectangular route is a 2:1 top-hat imposed inlet, not an internal nozzle |
| `legacy-scalar-reel-v31` | Field diagnostics | Existing phase/speed/vorticity reel | Separates scalar evidence from surface rendering | No pressure panel; older restored pressure is not usable |
| `complex-geometry-contact-sheet-v3` | Geometry inspection | Existing compact flythrough contact sheet | Adds multiview geometry inspection without another long video | Rendered inspection product; not independent validation |
| `quarter-mirrored-no-go` | Negative diagnostic | `proxies/quarter_mirrored_render_only.mp4` | Makes the failed acceleration route and rendering boundary visible | One simulated quadrant mirrored; not full-domain physics; no breakup evidence |
| `internal-nozzle-l7-native-vof` | New route evidence | `proxies/internal_nozzle_l7_uninterrupted_native_vof.mp4` | Distinct full-domain L7 native VOF sequence | Uninterrupted reference only; connected waviness, no atomisation/breakup |
| `route-decision-summary` | Decision map | `proxies/route_decision_summary.svg` | Compresses keep/close/hold choices into one review view | Status summary, not quantitative validation |
| `internal-nozzle-evidence-summary` | Pressure/geometry summary | `proxies/internal_nozzle_l7_evidence_summary.svg` | Shows exact source-derived pressure range trend and final bounded metrics | Uninterrupted reference only; no exact-time restored-field comparison |

Every asset's checksum, source branch/SHA, caption, alt text, allowed claims,
and prohibited claims are recorded in `ARTIFACT_MANIFEST.json`.
