
# Claim Boundary

Allowed internal wording:

- Long official Basilisk pulsed-jet control benchmark candidate.
- Modified 2:1 rectangular imposed-inlet benchmark comparison.
- Atomisation-style benchmark evidence pending human review.
- Thresholded topology/component diagnostics.
- Solver-derived native VOF/facet and Blender/Cycles review media.

Not allowed:

- Validation, production CFD, experimental agreement, or pressure-atomized-nozzle validation.
- Stationary spray unless a later quantified stationary window is demonstrated and reviewed.
- Internal-nozzle atomisation for the rectangular route.
- Droplet statistics, D32/SMD conclusions, or fit readiness from the thresholded component gate.
- Public readiness or portfolio publication before a separate human-reviewed publication task.

Persistent batch flags: `fit_ready=false`, `public_ready=false`.
