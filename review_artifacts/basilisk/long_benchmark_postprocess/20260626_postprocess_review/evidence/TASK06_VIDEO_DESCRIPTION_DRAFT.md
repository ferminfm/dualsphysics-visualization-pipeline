# Draft Video Description

Do not publish yet. This copy is for Layer-1/human review only.

This internal benchmark visualizes a dimensionless Basilisk two-phase VOF liquid-jet calculation. The lead route is the official circular pulsed control; a secondary 2:1 area-matched rectangular top-hat imposed-inlet case is shown as a caveated comparison.

Important caveats:
- The rectangular case imposes a top-hat velocity at the inlet plane and does not resolve internal nozzle flow.
- The rectangular route is resolution-sensitive and secondary.
- Field visualizations are diagnostic post-processing outputs from restored exports.
- Pressure visualization is blocked because restored pressure exports were zero-range in the selected checkpoints.
- This is not validation, not production CFD, not a stationary spray result, not an experimental comparison, and not fit-ready.

Suggested title if a future public packaging task approves it:
Basilisk VOF Jet Benchmark: Circular Lead and Rectangular Imposed-Inlet Comparison
