## Candidate Title
Basilisk Long Two-Phase Spray Benchmark: Circular Lead With Rectangular Imposed-Inlet Comparison

## Draft Summary
This internal review packet presents a no-gravity, dimensionless Basilisk VOF benchmark using the official circular pulsed liquid-jet route as the lead visual/scientific candidate. A secondary 2:1 area-matched rectangular top-hat imposed-inlet comparison is included only as a caveated resolution-sensitive comparison. The rectangular case does not resolve internal nozzle flow and should not be described as a selected Poiseuille route.

## What Is Shown
- Full-length official circular control sequence through the selected long window.
- Time-aligned circular versus rectangular top-hat comparison.
- Diagnostic field visualizations for phase, speed, finite-difference vorticity magnitude, and ambient-VOF complement from restored exports.
- Corrected Blender v2/v3 visual assets and contact sheets for internal review.

## What Is Not Claimed
No validation, production CFD, stationary spray, experimental agreement, true atomisation, pressure-atomized-nozzle validation, fit-ready, or public-ready claim is made.

## Required Caveat
The rectangular velocity profile is imposed at the inlet plane; internal nozzle flow is not resolved. Poiseuille-series profiles were implemented and tested in the broader route, but the bounded gate selected `C1_rect_area_top_hat`, not a Poiseuille-profile rectangular production route.
