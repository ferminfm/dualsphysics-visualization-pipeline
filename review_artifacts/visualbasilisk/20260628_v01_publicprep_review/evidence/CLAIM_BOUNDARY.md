# Claim Boundary

Allowed after review:

- Basilisk VOF-to-Blender bridge.
- Source-first scientific visualization workflow utility.
- Manifest/facet checks and review-packet helper.
- Technical sample or reproducible snapshot.

Not allowed:

- validation;
- production CFD;
- experimental agreement;
- stationary spray;
- true atomisation prediction;
- pressure-atomized-nozzle validation;
- final predictive modeling;
- public-ready release without separate human approval.
