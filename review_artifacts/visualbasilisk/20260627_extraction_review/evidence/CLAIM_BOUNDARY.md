# Claim Boundary

Allowed internal wording:

- Basilisk VOF-to-Blender bridge.
- Source-first scientific visualization workflow utility.
- Manifest/facet checking and review-packet helper.
- Frozen tested snapshot for human review.

Prohibited wording:

- validation;
- production CFD;
- experimental agreement;
- stationary spray;
- true atomisation prediction;
- pressure-atomized-nozzle validation;
- final predictive modeling;
- public-ready package;
- claims that rectangular imposed-inlet evidence resolves internal-nozzle flow.
